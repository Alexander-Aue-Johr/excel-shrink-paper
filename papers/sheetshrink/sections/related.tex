\subsection{Spreadsheet loading and parsing}
SheetReader studies specialized spreadsheet ingestion and the coupling of parsing,
decompression and parallel execution~\cite{sheetreader}. It motivates evaluating
loading costs and memory while providing a different output contract. SheetShrink
produces a transformed workbook and must account for retained layout effects.
The evaluation should measure whether cleanup also benefits downstream readers.

\subsection{Specialized semi-structured execution}
Mison combines structural indexing with speculative access to queried JSON
fields~\cite{mison}. Simdjson uses SIMD operations in a validating JSON
parser~\cite{simdjson}. These systems establish specialized parsing as a
substantial data-management topic. SheetShrink adds a document-rewriting
obligation involving workbook dependencies and native layout. Its evaluation
must account for decompression, output construction and subsequent use of the
cleaned workbook, alongside byte-processing cost.

\subsection{Spreadsheet analysis and testing}
Spreadsheet slicing integrates dependencies with testing and debugging feedback
in a spreadsheet environment~\cite{slicing}. Its treatment of indirect effects
helps motivate dependency guards, while the preservation task here additionally
includes native layout and graphical presentation. Workbook difference tools
such as Spreadsheet Compare offer formula and format categories~\cite{spreadsheetcompare}.
Their documented capabilities are evaluated as distinct observations rather than
assumed to constitute a pixel-rendering oracle.
