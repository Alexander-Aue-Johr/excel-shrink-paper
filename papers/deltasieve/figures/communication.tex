\caption{DeltaSieve communication and supervision, illustrated schematically.
(A) Each Excel session holds a comparison workbook and its capture add-in.
The default image transport uploads finalized PNGs and returns image
receipts before acknowledging completed commands. The capture service exposes
durable results to the comparison coordinator. Equal captures remain inspectable in the report.
Packaging accepts a smaller lossless PNG encoding only after decoded RGBA
equality is verified. Differing or unverified image references preserve their
source encoding. Startup is abstracted.
(B) An add-in can remain reachable while verified work stalls. The separate
parent-runner heartbeat expiry can trigger owned cleanup. Process exit still
requires confirmation. (C) A fast original client retains pending work while
its peer catches up. Credits cover issued work through comparison. PNG buffers
have additional byte limits and explicit upload/discard lifetimes.
Times and occupancy traces are illustrative.}
