The fast production strategy opens original and transformed workbooks in separate
owned Excel processes and rebuilds them in place. A configured worker pool may
lease existing processes across file pairs. Measurements must distinguish
per-workbook opening from process and add-in initialization, and record the
reuse policy when reporting campaign throughput. It compares their rebuilt
states. Saved-input and pre-rebuild controls can explain changes introduced by
Excel's own save or calculation behavior. Enabling additional controls incurs
separate cost and must be recorded in the run configuration. A cached control
is reusable only under its source and environment compatibility requirements.

Legacy XLS conversion precedes the XLSX comparison subject. A workbook requiring
repair, inaccessible protected content or an unsuccessful save receives its own
outcome. Missing evidence cannot yield equality. A host process existing in the
operating system and an Office.js add-in responding are separate liveness signals.
Neither proves that calculation or capture work has advanced.

\subsection{Select potentially affected regions}
The XML analysis compares cell values and formulas together with their effective
style context. A style index is local to a package. The same index can identify
changed properties, while different indices can identify equivalent properties.
The planner resolves that context before interpreting cell differences.

Layout analysis includes row, column and default-format effects. Stored row-height
attributes alone do not establish native effective heights. The native stage
therefore measures the relevant geometry. Content bounds include values,
formulas, visible fills and borders, and supported object extents. The union of
both states is important when an operation removes content.

Conditional-formatting analysis follows supported references. When a referenced
input changes, its dependent application region becomes a candidate regardless
of whether a particular rule outcome might remain the same. Unchanged supported
rules and inputs can exclude a region. Unknown formulas, namespaces or extensions
retain the documented fallback. Chart selection similarly considers supported
formulas, referenced inputs and caches, with conservative handling of unresolved
cases. The rule inventory and its unsupported cases are versioned with the tool.

\subsection{Measure and render}
Candidate regions are clipped to the justified content envelope. Connected regions
are subdivided according to native measured image size. A fixed row count cannot
predict the visible size of hidden or irregular rows. Zero-area regions have an
explicit non-rendered outcome. Geometry and image batches have separate controls,
allowing measurement overhead and rendering memory to be tuned independently.

Office.js image results become available after synchronization. Hash calculation
therefore follows synchronization of the requested image batch. Compatible equal
hashes can avoid transmitting and retaining equal images under the recorded
transport policy. Actual unequal images remain evidence. Hash transport, image
retention and scientific coverage are separate choices.

\subsection{Keep exclusion and capture geometry consistent}
The content envelope is a union over the inspected raw, saved and rebuilt
states. Supported visible formatting contributes alongside values and formulas.
An operation that removes content therefore cannot reduce the plan merely by
making the transformed package smaller. Native object observations and
unsupported dependencies retain their own coverage rules.

Height dependencies stop at the last row in this union. Policy v12 applies the
same lower boundary to cell capture rectangles~\cite{trailingrowvalidation}.
Context above the content and on either side remains available. Empty rows below
the union cannot move content above them, whereas an empty row preceding later
content can change its position. The boundary is recomputed through the existing
state analysis. A later relevant extent enlarges it before capture selection.
The planner reuses its stored rectangle and substitutes its unpadded bottom
coordinate, adding no Excel operation or second worksheet scan.

This rule concerns the supported relevant-content envelope. It does not remove
the need to model overflow, formatting or graphical objects correctly. Charts
retain separate observations. A native zero-width or zero-height range has an
explicit non-rendered outcome with geometry retained. A transition between
visible and non-rendered states remains a difference. Agreement of two
non-rendered ranges does not become a pixel-equality observation.

For captured images, raw dimensions and content origins remain available.
Eligible exterior-size changes can be distinguished from changed overlapping
pixels through the documented containment rules. A shifted origin, missing
measurement or changed content pixel retains the applicable difference or
incomplete outcome. Extent exclusion and exact white-margin exclusion have
separate receipt flags. A global tolerance would erase that distinction.

\subsection{Bounded execution and recovery}
Resident workers receive commands over session-scoped WebSockets, while image
uploads use bounded HTTP requests~\cite{deltasieveexecution}. Durable commands,
image receipts and results remain authoritative across reconnections. Duplicate
delivery must preserve an in-progress command and acknowledge an identical
completed result without running it again. Capture and upload overlap within
bounded tile, pixel and encoded-memory windows. Office calls remain serial
within an individual workbook.

Each comparison group owns its Excel processes and an isolated WebView profile.
Cleanup records requested termination separately from confirmed exit. A retained
clean-desktop control reproduced a peer taskpane restart under shared-profile
cleanup; per-group isolation passed the corresponding two-group control and a
four-group sequence. This observation motivates resource ownership in the tested
environment. It does not establish that every startup delay is caused by stale
profiles or registrations.

\subsection{Separate control traffic from retained evidence}
The resident command channel and the live status display have distinct roles.
WebSocket delivery supports prompt command and completion notification, with
durable records supporting reconciliation after disconnection. The Observatory
status stream starts with a snapshot and sends a restricted JSON Patch subset
over allowed paths. Sequence gaps require resynchronization. Image uploads and
detailed evidence remain HTTP operations~\cite{livestreamprotocol}.

A retained 100-card fixture records a 1,627,309-byte full status response,
a 17,722-byte changed response and a 1,415-byte unchanged-comparison response.
These measurements characterize status payloads for that fixture. They do not
measure sustained comparison throughput. Bounded image queues and hash-based
transport address separate costs and retain their own resource accounting.
Neither a smaller status response nor a delivered heartbeat establishes that
Excel has completed a pending render.

\subsection{Retain outcomes and explain uncertainty}
The report records selected regions, compared states, configuration, timings and
failures. Pixel diagnostics provide changed-pixel counts, channel deltas and
magnified regions. They do not assign a regression verdict on their own. Formula
dependency traces can identify a supported path to a volatile function without
explaining every changed pixel in the surrounding image.

Portable reports retain images using relative paths and integrity manifests.
Their static viewer needs no Excel installation or running control API. Run
metadata must be reviewed for local paths and publication-sensitive content
before public release. Historical replay requires recorded event history and
cannot reconstruct observations that were never retained.

\subsection{Collect independent runs in a static atlas}
Each analysis can publish a static report with a compressed catalog, per-run
detail and relative image assets~\cite{staticatlas}. A collection groups
workbooks by declared corpus, technical name and original content identity.
Processor versions and source runs keep separate identities. Missing input
identity prevents automatic grouping. A merge checks retained evidence and
preserves source provenance rather than collapsing independent observations
into one synthetic run.

The viewer retains the full worksheet inventory, including hidden and empty
sheets, while loading detail and images on demand. Zoom changes the view over
the same workbook and sheet hierarchy. Uncaptured regions have no reconstructed
image. Different processors share an inspection context only when the retained
original image, range, state, geometry and validation signature match. Native
image pixels are inspected without rescaling. A changed capture policy can
therefore leave an older processor without a matching context even when both
runs concern the same workbook.

Compressed JSON is decoded in the browser, and the exported site needs static
HTTP hosting. Integrity manifests retain report and image identities. Visual
markings expose recorded differences and incomplete evidence; they do not
replace the scientific coverage statement. The atlas supports inspection of
the same frozen outputs that can subsequently be revalidated with the native
tool. It introduces no additional detection claim.


\subsection{Bound uniform backgrounds across column-width changes}
A solid inherited fill spanning every worksheet column has no finite right edge.
Repeated column definitions can partition that same fill solely because their
widths differ. The planner resolves the existing column-format map and excludes
those width-only boundaries from the background envelope when every column
applies the same supported solid fill without borders. The existing byte scan
supplies the column and style records. This guard adds neither another package
read nor an Office.js synchronization.

Explicit cells, including blank overrides, remain in the envelope. Row-format
transitions and finite content or merge extents remain bounded observations.
Supported drawing bounds and conditional-formatting eligibility retain their
independent checks. A differing fill, a border, an unknown style or unsupported
worksheet context retains the applicable conservative extent. Column widths
within the retained content still participate in layout comparison. The guard
therefore removes redundant background boundaries while preserving the
observations used to detect interior changes.


An empty native \texttt{autoFilter} declaration is bounded separately. With a
valid explicit cell rectangle, no filter children and only supported reference
and revision-identifier attributes, its drop-down buttons remain within that
rectangle. The planner includes this rectangle before applying the background
proof. Actual filter criteria, sort children, unknown attributes and unsupported
namespace contexts retain the conservative path. Native row geometry remains
an independent observation. This distinction prevents a bounded button region
from expanding an otherwise uniform background to every column.


The idle watchdog counts validated native row-height replies as geometry
progress. The counter advances only after the returned height runs cover the
requested spans and pass the numeric and ordering checks. Cached lookups and
ordinary process or add-in heartbeats do not advance it. A workbook with many
small sheets can therefore continue through a long measurement phase while a
stalled native command remains bounded by its operation and idle deadlines.


Legacy drawing colour metadata is admitted only within a supported RGB colour
node. The accepted extension declares the Office 2010 drawing namespace,
marks that namespace as ignorable, and supplies a spreadsheet palette index
between 0 and 65. The RGB value remains the drawing colour. Unknown namespaces,
other attributes and out-of-range indices retain conservative coverage. The
existing native frame, line width, transform and effect checks still determine
whether the line admits bounded capture coverage.

Opening recovery retains explicit input provenance. Some converted Destatis
workbooks contain both a legacy print-area or print-title name and the canonical
built-in name in the same worksheet scope. Excel displays a name-conflict
prompt. For a controlled recovery baseline, we rename the legacy alias only
when exactly one canonical peer exists and package inspection finds no formula
or relationship reference to that alias. Both definitions and their formulas
remain present. Only the defined-name attribute in the workbook XML changes.
Macro-bearing inputs and referenced aliases require separate review. Raw,
normalized and transformed SHA-256 identities are retained with the changed
member and alias mapping. Recovery results are labelled as comparisons against
this normalized baseline.


Confirmed setup-phase completions also advance the idle-progress counter.
Opening, saving and XML inspection may together exceed the idle interval while
each phase completes successfully. A transition to a completed phase advances
the counter once. Repeating the same completed status leaves it unchanged.
Running statuses and liveness samples retain their existing watchdog semantics.


A supported horizontal line can have a different native vertical position from
its serialized absolute transform after Excel resolves row anchors. Qualification
requires a unique matching name, unrotated horizontal geometry, identical left
coordinate and width, and a finite nonnegative native top coordinate. A single
capture rectangle must contain the union of the native and stored positions,
including half the stroke width and two points of rasterization context. A
position outside that padded rectangle or a changed width retains the fallback.
The resulting region is captured in both comparison states.


Capture policy version 13 reconstructs the visual-content envelope from the
states actually compared after rebuilding. Native-qualified drawing bounds
therefore replace earlier unqualified control bounds in this envelope.
Opening/saving and input-transformation controls retain their XML change
records and input identities. Their formatting extents do not enlarge the
rebuilt-state image plan. The coverage claim is relative to the compared rebuilt
states and the recorded selected regions.
