# XLSX growth audit

The first five transferred original/output pairs passed the requested SHA-256 and byte-size checks. Every ZIP member was read completely, hashed, and compared by compressed size, uncompressed size and compression method. All member payloads were also compressed with the same Python zlib implementation using raw DEFLATE levels 2 and 6. Streaming inspection retains small buffers and creates no replacement XLSX files.

All five outputs have smaller total uncompressed payloads and smaller total XML payloads. Their compressed payloads grow. Archive overhead decreases by 240 to 1,904 bytes. Recompressing both sides with the same implementation and level produces smaller output payload totals in every case, at both tested levels. The growth in these five pairs is attributable to the actual compression outcome.

The largest case is the Data.gov Peanut Core Collection. Its XML shrinks by 2,700 bytes while the archive grows by 12,179,635 bytes. `xl/worksheets/sheet4.xml` and `sheet5.xml` each shrink by 36 uncompressed bytes. Their original compressed sizes are 10,594,365 and 10,630,970 bytes, while their output compressed sizes are 16,678,526 and 16,728,672 bytes. Both archives use DEFLATE for those entries. Common zlib level 2 yields essentially equal sizes for the before/after payload of each of these two sheets. Common level 6 compresses each to approximately 8 MB.

Detailed per-member results and the counterfactual size decomposition are under `results/`. `summary.csv` contains the aggregate results. The three additional Destatis pairs were subsequently transferred and verified. Their total XML grows by 7,969, 2,179 and 2,793 bytes. Growing sheet3.xml parts have 399, 661 and 685 additional row style/customFormat attributes while their cell counts decrease by 50, 8 and 38, respectively. Their archive growth remains dominated by compression differences. The audit directory contains all eight complete pair results. The 771-of-774 preliminary count covers the worksheet/workbook parts in the full analysis ledger. The five transferred-pair conclusions cover every member of the exact XLSX archives.
