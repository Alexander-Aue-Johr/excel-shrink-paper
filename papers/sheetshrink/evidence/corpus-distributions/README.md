# Uncompressed corpus distributions

The figure uses the verified full-supplied-XLSX distribution series. The processed populations are 1,182 Destatis, 30,301 prepared Enron XLSX, and 8,623 Data.gov workbooks. Data.gov has 494 unsupported-namespace exclusions. Per-workbook payload sizes come from every ZIP central-directory member's uncompressed size. Their source/output identity multisets match the processed rows in each original transfer table, including multiplicity. The collector was checked against complete decompression of all eight growth-audit pairs.

The worksheet panels use per-part uncompressed XML sizes and cover 32,832 Destatis, 120,740 Enron and 46,340 Data.gov worksheets. Eighty-eight prepared Enron workbooks have no worksheet XML observations. The workbook denominator includes those workbooks. Per-cause rows are reduced to one size observation per worksheet before plotting.

All nine axes share a display endpoint at the 85th percentile. The endpoint is the largest corpus/metric percentile attaining at least 0.5 percentage points of positive reduction, rounded up to a multiple of five. Remaining positive reductions are smaller than that threshold. The omitted tail also contains unchanged outputs and growth. Growth counts are 3/24/5 workbooks and 82/143/39 worksheets in Destatis/Enron/Data.gov order. The underlying tables retain the complete populations and signed changes.

Reduction classes are below 50%, 50–80%, 80–90%, 90–95%, and at least 95%. The figure reuses the earlier light blue, yellow, orange and vermilion palette and adds a dark red high-reduction class. Hatching, distinct line patterns, marker shapes and dark outlines carry information alongside color. A grayscale proof was inspected. IEEE recommends combining color with shape or fill patterns, checking grayscale interpretation, and differentiating brightness. Its guidance does not prescribe this palette. See https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/create-graphics-for-your-article/ and https://dis.acm.org/2023/creating-accessible-figures-and-tables/.

Reproduce the final figure with `scripts/analyze_enron_xlsx_files/plot_uncompressed_distributions.py`, passing this evidence directory, the original transferred v2 package root, and an output directory. The transferred package root contains the full `workbooks.csv` identity tables and `uncompressed-workbooks.csv` measurements. The compressed tables retained here provide the numeric plotting observations. `uncompressed-coverage.json` records the display policy, counts, tiers and growth extrema. `*-inventory.json` identifies the original full distribution packages, and `*-run.json` records their builds and configurations.
