### Repeated cache-admission explanation

> The cache admission condition can be expressed as compatibility of four
> independent facts. The previous observation must be complete, its required
> package contents must match, its selected component set must cover the new
> request, and its renderer/preparation policy must remain compatible. Matching
> only a filename or producer binary is insufficient. Matching workbook bytes
> without the renderer contract is likewise insufficient for a native result.
> A changed compression level can preserve member identity, while a new ordinary
> save can change it and invalidate admission.

## Next candidate 1 - preliminary Intel measurement lane

### Earlier stream results

> The Figure~\ref{fig:cell-record} implementation passed 6,561 ordered comparisons
> against an independent coordinate/value oracle. Twenty-seven focused tests
> covered cell streams, XML scanning and style identities. A preliminary release-build
> experiment used three fresh processes per case, with normal filesystem caching.
> For two inputs of one million cells each, changing every value and consuming
> changes immediately produced a peak process working set of 4.52\,MiB.
> Retaining all emitted change records increased this to 93.78\,MiB,
> giving a 95.2\% reduction for immediate consumption. These are the largest
> process peaks across the three trials. The comparison isolates result retention;
> the full pipeline retains additional change metadata.
>
> Both modes wrote 80\,MB of scratch records and took approximately 2.1 seconds
> to build the two streams. Median merge times were 70.2\,ms with immediate
> consumption and 113.0\,ms with retention. Across three Enron worksheets with
> 3.06--6.49 million cells, single-value mutations each yielded exactly one changed
> cell. The largest pair required 15.33 seconds to build its streams, 419\,ms to
> merge them and 519\,MB of scratch space, with a 4.74\,MiB peak working set.
> ZIP decompression and input preparation precede these timers. Filesystem-cache
> memory is outside the process counters. These observations characterize the
> streaming component and its result-storage trade-off.

### Retain

Keep the completed Ryzen follow-up and its receipt citation. It repeats the
63-trial protocol and 27 focused tests, reports a 94.0% dense-change working-set
reduction, and preserves the 42.121 MiB real-input outlier. Move the earlier Intel
numbers and environment to their existing evidence artifact. Keep one compact
sentence explaining the coordinate/value oracle and the result-retention scope.
A disk-versus-heap comparison is needed only for a broader representation claim.
